<PageHeader slug="questionnaire-drafts" title="Drafts & History" component="questionnaire">
  Nothing typed is lost in a session. The walk - the step, every answer, the history of steps taken, what was skipped - is a <code>persisted()</code> store, written on every keystroke and read back when the page loads: a reload, a crash, an accidental close all come back to the same question with the same answers. Back goes back along the branch history, not to the previous fieldset; the trail and the review's Edit jump to any step taken.
</PageHeader>

<H2 id="draft">The draft</H2>

<DocText>By default the draft lives in <strong>session storage</strong> - this tab, until it closes - under <code>defuss-shadcn:&lt;page path&gt;:questionnaire:&lt;id&gt;</code>. <code>data-persist="local"</code> keeps it across visits, <code>data-persist="none"</code> nowhere; <code>data-persist-prefix</code> / <code>data-persist-key</code> (or <code>configure(form, \{ persist \})</code>) name the place. A restored draft says so, with Start over. Sending clears it.</DocText>

<H2 id="history">Branch history and back navigation</H2>

<DocText>The history is the list of steps taken - on a branching flow that is not the order of the markup. <strong>Back</strong> walks it backwards and keeps the steps ahead: going forward again without changing an answer follows the same way. Change an answer and the history ahead is cut at that step (and answers the new way no longer reaches are <a class="link" href="questionnaire-dependencies.html">cleared</a>). The <code>.questionnaire-trail</code> - inside the form or anywhere with <code>data-for="form-id"</code> - lists the steps with their answers; each is a button back. The review's <strong>Edit</strong> does the same.</DocText>

<H2 id="api">In code</H2>

<DocText><code>df$.shadcn.questionnaire</code> has <code>back(form)</code>, <code>next(form)</code>, <code>skip(form)</code>, <code>goTo(form, step)</code> (a step of the history), <code>restart(form)</code>, <code>history(form)</code> and <code>answers(form)</code>; the walk is <code>form.store.value.config</code> - <code>\{ step, answers, history, index, skipped \}</code> - and <code>questionnaire-step</code> fires on every move.</DocText>
