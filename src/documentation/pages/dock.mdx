<PageHeader slug="dock" title="Dock" component="dock">
  A bottom navigation bar for the few top-level places of an app - an icon over a label per item, the active one highlighted. Fixed to the bottom and clear of the phone's home indicator by default; links mark the active page with <code>aria-current</code>, and a dock of radio labels switches with no JavaScript at all. daisyUI's dock, plus colors, a floating pill, badges, a raised action, label modes and a magnify effect.
</PageHeader>

<SkillPanel component="dock">
  <SkillField label="Native basis">
<SkillText><code>&lt;nav&gt;</code> of links, buttons or radio labels; the active item is <code>aria-current</code> / <code>aria-pressed</code> / a checked radio (<code>:has()</code>).</SkillText>
  </SkillField>

  <SkillField label="Web Platform APIs">
<SkillRow>
    <ApiPill href="https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Accessibility/ARIA/Attributes/aria-current" title="the active link"><code>aria-current</code></ApiPill>
    <ApiPill href="https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/:has" title="a checked radio makes its item active"><code>:has()</code></ApiPill>
    <ApiPill href="https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/env" title="clear of the home indicator"><code>env(safe-area-inset-bottom)</code></ApiPill>
    <ApiPill href="https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/backdrop-filter" title="the glass surface"><code>backdrop-filter</code></ApiPill>
  </SkillRow>
  </SkillField>

  <SkillField label="Classes">
<SkillRow>
    <code>.dock</code>
    <code>.dock-item</code>
    <code>.dock-icon</code>
    <code>.dock-label</code>
    <code>.dock-badge</code>
    <code>.dock-dot</code>
    <code>.dock-view</code>
    <code>.dock-panels</code>
    <code>.dock-panel</code>
  </SkillRow>
  </SkillField>

  <SkillField label="Data attributes">
<SkillText><code>data-variant</code> (muted, primary, neutral, glass, floating), <code>data-size</code> (xs … xl), <code>data-indicator</code> (bar, pill, dot), <code>data-labels</code> (active, none), <code>data-position</code> (top, left, right, static), <code>data-attach="container"</code>, <code>data-effect="magnify"</code>, <code>data-autohide</code>, <code>data-raised</code> on an item (a FAB there opens a semicircle dial).</SkillText>
  </SkillField>
</SkillPanel>
