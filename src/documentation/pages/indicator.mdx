<PageHeader slug="indicator" title="Indicator" component="indicator">
  Pins a badge, a status dot or a button to any of nine points of an element - a count on a button, a dot on a card, a "Required" tag on an input. <code>.indicator</code> wraps the element, <code>.indicator-item</code> sits centered on its anchor point, and <code>.indicator-dot</code> is the status dot, with ping, bounce and pulse animations. No JavaScript.
</PageHeader>

<h2 id="status" class="h2-display">Status dots</h2>

<DocText><code>.indicator-dot</code> is a small colored dot for presence and state - inline next to a label, or pinned as an <code>.indicator-item</code>. Give it <code>role="img"</code> and an <code>aria-label</code>, and pair it with text where there is room: color alone is not a message.</DocText>

```html example label="Status" hint="An inline dot next to its label."
<p class="m-0 flex items-center gap-2 text-sm"><span class="indicator-dot" data-variant="success" role="img" aria-label="Online"></span> All systems operational</p>
```
