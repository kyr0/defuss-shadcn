<H2 id="flow">Flow</H2>

<DocText>Every edge flows at once: <code>data-flow</code> (ms a pass), <code>data-flow-tokens</code> (tokens on the wire) and <code>data-flow-delay</code> (when this wire starts) hand the stream from one connection to the next. Tokens never run under reduced motion.</DocText>
