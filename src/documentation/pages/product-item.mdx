<PageHeader slug="product-item" title="Product Item" component="product-item">
  Displays one catalog entry with its image, name, price, and link - a second picture on hover, badges, rating, colors, a wishlist heart and add to cart. Part of the <DocLink href="website-shop.html">Shop</DocLink> blocks.
</PageHeader>

  <SkillField label="Native basis">
<SkillText>An <code>&lt;article&gt;</code>: two pictures (the second fades in on hover), a Badge, a wishlist checkbox, the name link, a description, stars (<code>--mk-rating</code>), the price with <code>&lt;del&gt;</code> for the old one, color swatches and an add-to-cart Button.</SkillText>
  </SkillField>
