<PageHeader slug="play-button" title="Play Button" component="play-button">
  A large round button that starts something - a video, a slide deck, content that loads only on demand (the <DocLink href="teaser.html">Teaser</DocLink>). One native <code>&lt;button&gt;</code> with an accessible name: the circle is its box, the triangle its <code>::before</code>, cut out with <code>clip-path</code>. No icon, no JavaScript.
</PageHeader>
