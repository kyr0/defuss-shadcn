<PageHeader slug="anim-canvas" title="Animation Canvas" component="anim-canvas">
  A chess-board slide canvas: slides sit side by side on one board, the viewport frames exactly one of them (1:1), arrow keys move directionally, and a zoomed-out <strong>overview</strong> shows the whole board at once - click a tile to zoom back in. Every slide transition is the shared <code>df$.anim</code> engine, declared in markup per slide; the canvas ships no animation of its own.
</PageHeader>

<p class="text-muted-foreground m-0">• Animations are engine names - see the <DocLink href="motion.html">Motion</DocLink> vocabulary and the per-animation deep dives (fade/slide/zoom/pop/spin/flip/skew/blur/wipe/iris + the blocks roll-over). • Only the slide you move to animates - the one you leave pans out of frame still. Its arrival starts as the camera reaches it, then its content builds in (<code>data-df-entrance</code> / <code>data-df-draw</code> / <code>data-count</code> replay through the shared motion controller). • <code>blocksIn</code> makes the arriving slide land under a curtain that rolls off it - give it a visible <code>data-anim-in-color</code>. • <code>--anim-canvas-gap</code> (board units, default 80) separates the cells - the overview shows them as tiles. • Arrow keys never swallow a direction that has no neighbor.</p>

  <p class="text-sm text-muted-foreground m-0 mb-3">Named states via the shared State API, driven per instance through the bound <code>api</code>:</p>
  <ul class="text-sm text-muted-foreground m-0 mb-3 ps-5">
    <li><code>default</code> - one slide framed 1:1; <code>setState('default', \{ slide: 's2' \})</code> focuses that slide, bare <code>setState('default')</code> re-frames the active one</li>
    <li><code>overview</code> - the whole board zoomed out (<code>data-overview</code>); every slide is a clickable tile that zooms back in</li>
  </ul>
  <p class="text-sm text-muted-foreground">The demo card carries <code>data-state-demo</code>; <code>bun run screenshots</code> drives it via <code>el.api.setState(name)</code>.</p>

| `overview` | `boolean` | `true`, `false` | `false` | Zoomed-out board view (`data-overview` on the root) - `setState('overview')` / `setState('overview', { value: false })`. |
