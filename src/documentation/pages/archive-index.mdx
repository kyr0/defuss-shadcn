<PageHeader slug="archive-index" title="Archive Index" component="archive-index">
  Links to content grouped by publication year or month - native disclosures with post counts, or the years side by side. Part of the <DocLink href="website-blog.html">Blog</DocLink> blocks.
</PageHeader>

<SkillPanel component="archive-index">
  <SkillField label="Native basis">
<SkillText>A <code>&lt;nav&gt;</code> of <code>&lt;details name="archive"&gt;</code> disclosures - one per year, exclusive by the shared <code>name</code>, animated with <code>::details-content</code> - each a list of month links with post counts.</SkillText>
  </SkillField>

  <SkillField label="Built from">
<SkillText><DocLink href="timeline.html">Timeline</DocLink>, <DocLink href="badge.html">Badge</DocLink>.</SkillText>
  </SkillField>

  <SkillField label="Web Platform APIs">
<SkillRow>
    <ApiPill href="https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Element/details" title="exclusive year disclosures"><code>&lt;details name&gt;</code></ApiPill>
    <ApiPill href="https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/::details-content" title="the open / close animation"><code>::details-content</code></ApiPill>
    <ApiPill href="https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/interpolate-size" title="animates to auto height"><code>interpolate-size</code></ApiPill>
    <ApiPill href="https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Accessibility/ARIA/Attributes/aria-current" title="the month being shown"><code>aria-current</code></ApiPill>
  </SkillRow>
  </SkillField>

  <SkillField label="Classes">
<SkillRow>
    <code>.mk-archive-index</code>
    <code>.mk-archive-index-title</code>
    <code>.mk-archive-index-year</code>
    <code>.mk-archive-index-count</code>
    <code>.mk-archive-index-legend</code>
    <code>.mk-archive-index-tags</code>
    <code>.mk-archive-index-months</code>
    <code>.mk-archive-index-pick</code>
  </SkillRow>
  </SkillField>

  <SkillField label="Data attributes">
<SkillText><code>data-variant="columns"</code>; <code>open</code> and <code>name</code> on the year disclosures; <code>aria-current</code> on the current month; <code>data-variant="calendar"</code> (+ <code>data-level</code> 0-4 on the month links, <code>.mk-archive-index-legend</code>); <code>.mk-archive-index-tags</code> (+ <code>data-weight</code> 1-5), <code>.mk-archive-index-months</code>, <code>.mk-archive-index-pick</code>.</SkillText>
  </SkillField>
</SkillPanel>
