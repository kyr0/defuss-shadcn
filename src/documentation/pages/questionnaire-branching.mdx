<PageHeader slug="questionnaire-branching" title="Branching" component="questionnaire">
  A questionnaire is a graph, not a list. Where a step leads is decided, in order, by the chosen option's <code>data-goto</code>, the first conditional rule whose conditions hold, the step's <code>data-next</code>, and otherwise the next step in the markup. Rules read any earlier answer - numbers, choices, several answers at once, one answer compared with another - so the same form asks a developer, a designer and a team lead different questions.
</PageHeader>

<H2 id="choice-branches">Choice branches</H2>

<DocText>The simplest branch is the answer itself: <code>data-goto</code> on a radio, a checkbox or an <code>&lt;option&gt;</code> sends the walk to that step. When every option of a required single choice jumps, the step has no default way at all - the graph knows that, too.</DocText>

<H2 id="rule-branches">Rule branches</H2>

<DocText>Rules live in a <code>&lt;script type="application/json" class="questionnaire-rules"&gt;</code> inside the form (or come from <code>configure(form, \{ branches \})</code>). For each step a list of <code>\{ when, goto \}</code> - the first rule whose <code>when</code> holds wins. A <code>when</code> is a list of conditions that must all hold, or <code>\{ "any": [...] \}</code>. A condition is <code>\{ field, op, value \}</code> with dataview's operators plus <code>answered</code>, <code>empty</code> and <code>includes</code>; a <code>value</code> of <code>\{ "field": "x" \}</code> compares with another answer.</DocText>

<H2 id="order">The order of precedence</H2>

<DocText>For the step being left: (1) the picked option's <code>data-goto</code>; (2) the first rule in <code>branches[step]</code> whose <code>when</code> holds (an empty <code>when</code> always does - a catch-all); (3) <code>data-next</code>; (4) the next <code>.questionnaire-step</code> in the markup. Skipping an optional step takes (3) or (4) - a skipped answer cannot choose a branch. <code>df$.shadcn.questionnaire.nextOf(form, step, answers)</code> evaluates the same order for any answers, and <a class="link" href="questionnaire-graph.html">the flow graph page</a> draws every possible edge.</DocText>
