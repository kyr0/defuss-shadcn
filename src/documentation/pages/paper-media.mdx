  <section class="paper-abstract" aria-labelledby="pm-abstract">
    <h2 id="pm-abstract">Abstract</h2>
    <p>A figure in a paper should load lazily, keep its space while it loads, fail visibly and open larger on demand; a video should carry captions and chapters. Each of these is a native element with a few attributes. This paper uses three placeholder images - one scene in a neutral, a cool and a warm tone - and a six-second captioned clip.</p>
  </section>
