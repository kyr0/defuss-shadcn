<PageHeader slug="event-countdown" title="Event Countdown" component="event-countdown">
  Displays the remaining time until a specified event - days, hours, minutes and seconds ticking down beside the date and the action. Part of the <DocLink href="website-events-promotions.html">Events & Promotions</DocLink> blocks.
</PageHeader>

<SkillPanel component="event-countdown">
  <SkillField label="Native basis">
<SkillText>A <code>&lt;section&gt;</code> with a title, the date as <code>&lt;time&gt;</code>, a Countdown group (<code>.countdown-group[data-until]</code>) and an action. Without script the numbers stay at the authored values.</SkillText>
  </SkillField>

  <SkillField label="Built from">
<SkillText><DocLink href="countdown.html">Countdown</DocLink>, <DocLink href="button.html">Button</DocLink>.</SkillText>
  </SkillField>
