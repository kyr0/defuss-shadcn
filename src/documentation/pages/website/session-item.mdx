<PageHeader slug="session-item" title="Session Item" component="session-item">
  Presents one scheduled session with its time, topic, and speakers - with its track, room and an "add to my schedule" toggle; breaks and keynotes styled apart. Part of the <DocLink href="website-events-promotions.html">Events & Promotions</DocLink> blocks.
</PageHeader>
