<h2 id="method" class="h2-display">What each site carries</h2>

<DocText>The <DocLink href="website-showcase.html">Showcase</DocLink> method, applied to businesses whose sites have a job to do:</DocText>

<ul class="mb-8" style="max-width:46rem;line-height:1.7">
  <li><b>The task the visitor came for, working</b> - a table booked for a slot the kitchen serves, a price for your hair length, the nights of a stay with the city tax, an appointment request beside the emergency numbers, a deadline counted to the day.</li>
  <li><b>A detail only this business has</b> - allergen codes, processing times, room sizes in m², the out-of-hours number, sauna temperatures, the statutory fees, the weeks of a project, prices per million tokens.</li>
  <li><b>A Site Header and a Site Footer</b> - the navigation jumps to the sections, the footer holds the address, the hours and working imprint and privacy dialogs.</li>
  <li><b>Three looks, each in light and dark</b> - the Look select and the light / dark switch in the header.</li>
</ul>
