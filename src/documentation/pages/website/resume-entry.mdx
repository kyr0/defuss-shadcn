<PageHeader slug="resume-entry" title="Resume Entry" component="resume-entry">
  One line of a CV - a position, a degree, a talk - with when and where, the highlights and the skills it used; a promotion path at one employer, a timeline through the logos, or the dates in a column of their own. Part of the <DocLink href="website-resume-portfolio.html">Resume &amp; Portfolio</DocLink> blocks.
</PageHeader>

<SkillPanel component="resume-entry">
  <SkillField label="Native basis">
<SkillText>An <code>&lt;ol&gt;</code> of entries, newest first: the organisation's logo or initials, the title as a heading, the organisation, when (<code>&lt;time&gt;</code>) and where, a sentence, the highlights and the skills as lists; several roles at one employer as a nested <code>&lt;ol&gt;</code>.</SkillText>
  </SkillField>
