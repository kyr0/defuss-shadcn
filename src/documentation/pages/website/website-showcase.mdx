<h2 id="method" class="h2-display">The method</h2>

<DocText>A components library gives every site the same parts; what makes a site its own is a handful of decisions that break the grid on purpose, taken from the subject's world. Each site here starts from one subject and keeps to these rules:</DocText>

<ul class="mb-8" style="max-width:46rem;line-height:1.7">
  <li><b>A palette and two typefaces of its own</b>, set as tokens on the site's root - every component inside follows them.</li>
  <li><b>One moving surface</b> - a <DocLink href="shader.html">Shader</DocLink> or a textured surface - where the subject has one: steam over a cup, a starfield, contour lines, a turning record, a woven thread of light.</li>
  <li><b>A few irregular moves</b>: a speech bubble, something pinned at a slant, a stamp, a ticket, a number circled by hand - each carrying content, not decoration.</li>
  <li><b>A detail only this subject has</b>: altitudes and cupping scores, magnitudes and seeing in arc-seconds, boulder grades, catalogue numbers and run times, kilobytes and cold starts.</li>
  <li><b>Three looks</b> - the Look select swaps the palette, the shader's colours and the paper, so one structure shows three identities.</li>
</ul>
