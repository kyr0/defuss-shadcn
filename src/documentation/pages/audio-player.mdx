  <SkillField label="Native basis">
<SkillText>An <code>&lt;article&gt;</code>: cover art, the title, show or artist and a metadata line (<code>&lt;time&gt;</code>), an <code>&lt;audio controls preload="none"&gt;</code>, and a download link (<code>download</code>); show notes in <code>&lt;details&gt;</code>.</SkillText>
