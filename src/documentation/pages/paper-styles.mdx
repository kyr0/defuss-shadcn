  <section class="paper-abstract" aria-labelledby="ps-abstract">
    <h2 id="ps-abstract">Abstract</h2>
    <p>A research page, a journal article and a lab note share their parts: a title block, an abstract, numbered sections, tables. What differs is the house style. Modern Paper keeps the parts and lets one attribute choose the style, so an author can change the look of a finished paper without touching its markup.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="paper-section" aria-labelledby="ps-s1">
    <h2 id="ps-s1"><span class="paper-num">1</span>Modern</h2>
    <p>The default is a project page: a centred title block, serif headings over a sans body, ruled section heads and a tinted abstract with an accent edge.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="paper-section" aria-labelledby="ps-s2">
    <h2 id="ps-s2"><span class="paper-num">2</span>Classic</h2>
    <p>The classic style sets a printed journal: serif throughout, a narrower column set justified and hyphenated, section heads in small capitals without a rule, and an abstract set in from both sides.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="paper-section" aria-labelledby="ps-s3">
    <h2 id="ps-s3"><span class="paper-num">3</span>Minimal</h2>
    <p>The minimal style is a lab note: everything flush start, a sans title, the abstract under a hairline instead of a tint, no rules under the heads.</p>
  </section>
