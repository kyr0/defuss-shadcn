<PageHeader slug="article-header" title="Article Header" component="article-header">
  Presents an article's title, subtitle, author, and publication date - with a lead image and caption, centered, or as a cover. Part of the <DocLink href="website-article.html">Article</DocLink> blocks.
</PageHeader>

<SkillPanel component="article-header">
  <SkillField label="Native basis">
<SkillText>A <code>&lt;header&gt;</code> - category link, the title (<code>h1</code>), a subtitle, the byline (Avatar, author link, <code>&lt;time&gt;</code>, read time) and an optional <code>&lt;figure&gt;</code> lead image with its <code>&lt;figcaption&gt;</code>.</SkillText>
  </SkillField>

  <SkillField label="Built from">
<SkillText><DocLink href="avatar.html">Avatar</DocLink>, <DocLink href="breadcrumb.html">Breadcrumb</DocLink>, <DocLink href="badge.html">Badge</DocLink>, <DocLink href="button.html">Button</DocLink>, <DocLink href="swap.html">Swap</DocLink>, <DocLink href="progress.html">Progress</DocLink>.</SkillText>
  </SkillField>
