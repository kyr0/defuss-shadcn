<PageHeader slug="video-player" title="Video Player" component="video-player">
  Plays one video with playback controls and captions - native controls, a WebVTT captions track, title and details, chapters or a transcript. Part of the <DocLink href="website-media.html">Media</DocLink> blocks.
</PageHeader>

<SkillPanel component="video-player">
  <SkillField label="Native basis">
<SkillText>A <code>&lt;figure&gt;</code>: a <code>&lt;video controls preload="metadata" playsinline poster&gt;</code> with WebM and MP4 <code>&lt;source&gt;</code>s and a captions <code>&lt;track&gt;</code>, then a <code>&lt;figcaption&gt;</code> with the title, duration and date; optionally chapters or a transcript in <code>&lt;details&gt;</code>.</SkillText>
  </SkillField>

  <SkillField label="Web Platform APIs">
<SkillRow>

    <ApiPill href="https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Element/figure" title="media with its caption"><code>&lt;figure&gt;</code></ApiPill>
    <ApiPill href="https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Element/details" title="native disclosure, no script"><code>&lt;details&gt;</code></ApiPill>
