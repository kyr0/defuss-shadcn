<PageHeader slug="event-header" title="Event Header" component="event-header">
  Introduces an event with its title, date, venue, and primary action - on a gradient band, over a cover picture, or beside a poster. Part of the <DocLink href="website-events-promotions.html">Events & Promotions</DocLink> blocks.
</PageHeader>
