<PageHeader slug="doc-comments" title="Document Comments" component="doc-comments">
  A column of comments beside a document, the way a Google Doc has one: every comment is a <DocLink href="card.html">Card</DocLink> tied to a span of the text, which the runtime wraps in a mark of the comment's colour. Click the mark and the column scrolls to the card; click the card's excerpt and the document scrolls to the mark; the <DocLink href="button-group.html">ButtonGroup</DocLink> at the top walks the comments up and down. A comment can quote another span - clicking the quote scrolls there and flashes it - and Reply opens a <DocLink href="textarea.html">Textarea</DocLink> composer whose answer nests under the comment. The whole thing is one JSON model. The <DocLink href="scaffold-document-editor.html">Document Editor</DocLink> scaffold puts it next to an <DocLink href="editorjs.html">Editor.js</DocLink> document.
</PageHeader>
