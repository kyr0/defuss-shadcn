<PageHeader slug="shader-liquid" title="Liquid & Glass" component="shader">
  Surfaces that flow and bend light: blobs that merge, chrome that runs over a logo, water over a photo, reeded glass, and ink you can stir. The picture effects read a picture inside the shader - an inline SVG, or an image served with CORS.
</PageHeader>

## Metaballs

<DocText>Blobs drift and merge where their fields meet; each keeps its color and the merged surface blends them. The field's slope lights the surface, so the blobs read as glossy drops. With <code>data-interactive</code> one more blob follows the pointer.</DocText>

```html example label="Drops on violet" schema="none" previewStyle="align-items:stretch;" hint="Move the pointer over it. The controls beside it are generated from the effect: its lists, its parameters and its colors."
<div class="grid gap-4" style="grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(16rem, 1fr))">
  <div
    class="shader"
    id="metaballs-demo"
    data-effect="metaballs"
    data-interactive
    data-colors="#ff7a59 #ffc75f #f9f871 #ff5e78"
    data-background="#2b1055"
    aria-hidden="true"
    style="--shader-aspect: 16 / 10; border-radius: var(--radius)"
  ></div>
  <div class="shader-controls" data-shader-for="metaballs-demo"></div>
</div>
```

## Liquid metal

<DocText>Chrome stripes flow over a shape and bend at its bevel, with a colored fringe where the light splits. The shape is the picture inside the shader - its alpha, contained in the box - or a rounded square without one. The colors are the stripes of the environment it reflects.</DocText>

## Water

## Fluid

<DocText>A real fluid simulation - advection, vorticity and a pressure solve every frame, in half-float textures - that drops ink in the colors by itself and follows the pointer: drag across it to stir. It needs float render targets (WebGL2 with <code>EXT_color_buffer_float</code>); without them the poster stays.</DocText>

```html example label="Ink to stir" schema="none" previewStyle="align-items:stretch;" hint="Drag the pointer across it. The controls beside it are generated from the effect: its lists, its parameters and its colors."

## Ripple

<DocText>Rings on a water surface: a drop falls every few seconds on its own, and a press starts a ring where it lands (the last eight presses are the <code>u_taps</code> uniform). Each ring bends what lies below - a picture child, or a gradient of the palette - and catches a highlight. <code>data-drops="0"</code> leaves the surface still until it is touched.</DocText>

```html example label="Press the water" schema="none" previewStyle="align-items:stretch;" hint="Press anywhere on the water. The controls beside it are generated from the effect: its lists, its parameters and its colors."
