<PageHeader slug="shader-light" title="Light & Glow" component="shader">
  Effects that shine: rays for a logo reveal, an aurora behind a hero, a glow around a call to action, foil on a card. Light adds to its background - a dark one makes it glow, <code>data-background="transparent"</code> lets the page show through.
</PageHeader>

## God rays

<DocText>Rays fan out from an origin - above the top edge by default - in three layers of angular noise that drift at their own pace, fading with distance. With <code>data-interactive</code> the origin follows the pointer.</DocText>

```html example label="Rays from above" schema="none" previewStyle="align-items:stretch;" hint="Move the pointer over it. The controls beside it are generated from the effect: its lists, its parameters and its colors."
<div class="grid gap-4" style="grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(16rem, 1fr))">
  <div
    class="shader"
    id="god-rays-demo"
    data-effect="god-rays"
    data-interactive
    aria-hidden="true"
    style="--shader-aspect: 16 / 10; border-radius: var(--radius)"
  ></div>
  <div class="shader-controls" data-shader-for="god-rays-demo"></div>
</div>
```

## Pulsing border

<DocText>A ring on the edge of its box - or, as <code>data-variant="border"</code>, on its parent's edge with the parent's corner radius - with light spots running around it and a breathing glow. The inside stays clear.</DocText>

```html example label="A button and a field" schema="none" hint="The button glows around its own edge; the input glows while it has focus. The controls beside it are generated from the effect: its lists, its parameters and its colors."
<div class="grid gap-4" style="grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(16rem, 1fr))">
  <div
    class="grid gap-4 items-center"
    style="grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(12rem, 1fr)); padding: 1.5rem"
  >
    <button class="btn" type="button" data-size="lg">
      <span
        class="shader"
        id="pulsing-border-demo"
        data-variant="border"
        data-effect="pulsing-border"
        data-spots="2"
        data-colors="#f472b6 #facc15 #38bdf8"
        style="--shader-glow: 0.75rem"
        aria-hidden="true"
      ></span>
      Get early access
    </button>
    <label class="grid gap-2 focus-glow">
      <span class="label">Ask anything</span>
      <span style="display: block; position: relative; isolation: isolate; border-radius: var(--radius-md)">
        <span
          class="shader"
          data-variant="border"
          data-effect="pulsing-border"
          data-thickness="1.5"
          data-colors="#7c5cff #2bd9ff"
          style="--shader-glow: 0.75rem"
          aria-hidden="true"
        ></span>
        <input class="input" type="text" placeholder="Search the docs" />
      </span>
    </label>
  </div>
  <div class="shader-controls" data-shader-for="pulsing-border-demo"></div>
</div>
<style>
  .focus-glow .shader {

  }
  .focus-glow:focus-within .shader {
    opacity: 1;
  }
</style>
```

## Hologram

<DocText>Holographic foil: rainbow bands that shift with the viewing angle, a sheen that sweeps across, glints and faint scanlines. Over a picture inside the shader the foil lies on the photo; <code>data-interactive</code> tilts it with the pointer.</DocText>

```html example label="A foil portrait" schema="none" hint="Move the pointer over the card to tilt it. The controls beside it are generated from the effect: its lists, its parameters and its colors."
<div class="grid gap-4" style="grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(16rem, 1fr))">
  <div
    class="shader"
    id="hologram-demo"
    data-effect="hologram"
    data-interactive
    style="max-width: 22rem; border-radius: 1rem"

## Bokeh

<DocText>Out-of-focus lights: four layers of discs, each brighter at its rim like a lens aperture, drifting upward at different speeds and twinkling. The nearer a layer, the larger and softer its discs. Without <code>data-colors</code> it takes a warm and cool evening palette.</DocText>

```html example label="Lights out of focus" schema="none" previewStyle="align-items:stretch;" hint="The controls beside it are generated from the effect: its lists, its parameters and its colors."

## Lightning

<DocText>Bolts strike from the top, each on a new jagged path with a branch, flicker and fade, and light the clouds behind them. Most moments are dark - the flashes are what you see. The core and the halo are measured in pixels, so a bolt stays a sharp line at any size.</DocText>

```html example label="A thunderstorm" schema="none" previewStyle="align-items:stretch;" hint="The controls beside it are generated from the effect: its lists, its parameters and its colors."
