  One component for graphs too big to draw node by node. The <DocLink href="graph-explorer.html">Graph Explorer</DocLink> keeps a million nodes in a Web Worker, draws what is far away into one canvas and gives DOM only to the few hundred nodes worth reading - chips, cards, labels along the edges. You zoom into a cluster to enter it and out to leave it; search, an outline, details and relations sit wherever the page puts them. The <DocLink href="scaffold-knowledge-graph.html">Knowledge Graph</DocLink> scaffold puts them into a Border Layout of Panels and drills from a domain down to a dataset, with pages built from Chart, Geo Map and the other components.

<H2 id="how-it-works">How it works</H2>

<DocText>The worker builds the graph and owns everything that scales with it: the hierarchy of clusters, a layout per cluster (made when you first come near it), the cycles, and every frame's far field - the dots and bulk edges of all nodes in view, drawn into an <code>OffscreenCanvas</code>. With each frame it sends back which nodes are worth a chip or a card. The main thread keeps a pool of DOM slots for those, morphs a slot only when its markup changed, and commits the bitmap and the DOM in the same animation frame. However big the graph, the page holds at most 120 cards, 300 chips and 200 edge labels.</DocText>

