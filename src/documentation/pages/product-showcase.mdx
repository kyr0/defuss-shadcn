<SkillPanel component="product-showcase">
  <SkillField label="Native basis">
<SkillText><code>&lt;figure&gt;</code> wrapper with three stacked layers - poster <code>&lt;img&gt;</code>, <code>&lt;video controls&gt;</code>, and a circular play <code>&lt;button&gt;</code>. Clicking the button (or pressing Enter on it - it's a real button) flips <code>data-state</code> to <code>playing</code>: CSS hides the poster and button, reveals the video, and JS starts muted playback. From then on the UA renders play/pause, scrubber, volume, and fullscreen. Native pause flips the state back so the poster returns.</SkillText>
  </SkillField>

<StatesSection>
  <p class="text-sm text-muted-foreground m-0 mb-3">Named states via the shared State API, driven per instance through the bound <code>api</code>:</p>
  <ul class="text-sm text-muted-foreground m-0 mb-3 ps-5">
    <li><code>default</code> - poster + play button (the authored state)</li>
    <li><code>playing</code> - the poster and button hide (CSS via <code>data-state</code>), the video plays muted; pausing returns to the poster</li>
  </ul>
  <p class="text-sm text-muted-foreground">The demo element carries <code>data-state-demo</code>; <code>bun run screenshots</code> drives it via <code>el.api.setState(name)</code> and captures <code>screenshots/\{mode\}/product-showcase-\{state\}.png</code>.</p>

```states

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `playing` | `boolean` | `true`, `false` | `false` | Video playback (muted when driven headlessly); `default` pauses and rewinds. |
```
