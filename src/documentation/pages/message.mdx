<PageHeader slug="message" title="Message" component="message">
  One row of a conversation, where the pieces come together: an <DocLink href="avatar.html">avatar</DocLink>, the sender's name and time, the <DocLink href="bubble.html">bubble</DocLink> (or a file), delivery status and actions. Rows sit on the received or sent side, group so one sender shows one avatar and one name, and compose with <DocLink href="marker.html">markers</DocLink> into a whole thread. The avatar stays level with the bubble's bottom edge, above any footer. CSS only, after shadcn/ui's Message.
</PageHeader>

<SkillPanel component="message">

<SkillText>A <code>.message</code> row of a <code>.message-avatar</code> slot and a <code>.message-content</code> column (header, surface, footer); <code>&lt;time datetime&gt;</code> for times; a <code>.message-list</code> with <code>role="log"</code> is the thread.</SkillText>
