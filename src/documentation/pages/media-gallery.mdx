  Coordinates a collection of images with shared viewing controls - switch between grid, masonry and filmstrip, and open a slideshow of every picture. Part of the <DocLink href="website-media.html">Media</DocLink> blocks.

<SkillPanel component="media-gallery">
  <SkillField label="Native basis">
<SkillText>A <code>&lt;section&gt;</code>: a header with the count, a radio group for the layout (grid / masonry / filmstrip - <code>:has(:checked)</code> switches the list), a slideshow Button (<code>commandfor</code> → <code>&lt;dialog&gt;</code>), the pictures as a list of <code>&lt;figure&gt;</code>s, and the dialog with a <code>scroll-snap</code> track.</SkillText>
  </SkillField>
