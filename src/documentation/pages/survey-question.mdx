```html example label="Single choice" hint="A fieldset with its legend, the position as a native <progress>, radio cards (:has(:checked) highlights) and back / next." previewStyle="align-items:stretch;"
<form class="mk-survey-question" action="#" method="post">
  <div class="mk-survey-question-meta"><span>Question 3 of 8</span><progress class="progress" value="3" max="8" aria-label="Survey progress">3 of 8</progress></div>
  <fieldset>
    <legend>How big is your team?</legend>
    <label class="mk-survey-question-option"><input type="radio" name="team" value="0" required>Just me</label>
    <label class="mk-survey-question-option"><input type="radio" name="team" value="1" checked required>2–10 people</label>
    <label class="mk-survey-question-option"><input type="radio" name="team" value="2" required>11–50 people</label>
    <label class="mk-survey-question-option"><input type="radio" name="team" value="3" required>More than 50</label>
  </fieldset>
  <div class="mk-survey-question-actions"><button class="btn" data-variant="ghost" type="button">Back</button><button class="btn" type="submit">Next</button></div>
</form>
```

```html example label="Multiple choice" hint="data-variant='multiple': checkbox cards with a &quot;choose all that apply&quot; hint as the fieldset's description." previewStyle="align-items:stretch;"
<form class="mk-survey-question" data-variant="multiple" action="#" method="post">
  <div class="mk-survey-question-meta"><span>Question 4 of 8</span><progress class="progress" value="4" max="8" aria-label="Survey progress">4 of 8</progress></div>
  <fieldset aria-describedby="sq-hint">
    <legend>Which tools does your team use today?</legend>
    <p class="mk-survey-question-hint" id="sq-hint">Choose all that apply.</p>
    <label class="mk-survey-question-option"><input type="checkbox" name="tools" value="0" checked>Spreadsheets</label>
    <label class="mk-survey-question-option"><input type="checkbox" name="tools" value="1" checked>A wiki</label>
    <label class="mk-survey-question-option"><input type="checkbox" name="tools" value="2">A task tracker</label>
    <label class="mk-survey-question-option"><input type="checkbox" name="tools" value="3">Chat only</label>
  </fieldset>
  <div class="mk-survey-question-actions"><button class="btn" data-variant="ghost" type="button">Back</button><button class="btn" type="submit">Next</button></div>

```html example label="Scale" hint="data-variant='scale': five steps from strongly disagree to strongly agree - each option's label is its full meaning." previewStyle="align-items:stretch;"
<form class="mk-survey-question" data-variant="scale" action="#" method="post">

  </fieldset>
  <div class="mk-survey-question-actions"><button class="btn" data-variant="ghost" type="button">Back</button><button class="btn" type="submit">Next</button></div>
