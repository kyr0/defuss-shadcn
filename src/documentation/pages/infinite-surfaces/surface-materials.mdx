<PageHeader slug="surface-materials" title="3D Materials" component="surface">
  Depth without geometry. The <DocLink href="surface.html">Surface</DocLink> fakes relief with masks - rims and shadows are the tile moved toward or away from the light - and draws neon and outlines from the tile's own edges. The <DocLink href="shader.html">Shader</DocLink>'s <code>surface</code> effect builds a height field from each pattern's distance to its edges, takes its normals and lights it - so the light can follow the pointer, or <code>data-light</code> and <code>data-elevation</code> place it.
</PageHeader>

## Relief in CSS

<DocText>25 styles, each with every tile. Emboss, deboss, shadow, long-shadow, extrude, glass, metal, gold and holographic fall away from <code>--surface-light-angle</code> (135deg, the upper left); <code>--surface-bevel</code>, <code>--surface-tube</code> and <code>--surface-glow</code> tune the rims.</DocText>

## Lit per pixel

<DocText>28 materials on the GPU: <b>bevel</b> (raised tiles, lit, over a darker grout (default)), <b>flat</b> (the colors as they are), <b>emboss</b> (one color, the relief alone draws the pattern), <b>neon</b> (tubes along the lines and around the shapes, a glow on black), <b>glass</b> (tiles that bend what lies behind them), <b>chrome</b> (a mirror of a studio), <b>ceramic</b> (glazed tiles in matte grout), <b>paper</b> (a matte sheet, the pattern pressed and printed in), <b>holo</b> (thin-film colors that turn with the view), <b>quilted</b> (padded cells with a stitched seam), <b>metal</b> (brushed steel, the grain along the tile), <b>gold</b> (a warm mirror), <b>copper</b> (a reddish mirror with a green patina in the grooves), <b>wood</b> (rings across each tile, every tile cut its own way), <b>marble</b> (polished stone with veins), <b>stone</b> (rough, matte and speckled), <b>rubber</b> (dark and soft), <b>plastic</b> (a clear color with a sharp white highlight), <b>velvet</b> (dark facing you, bright at the grazing edges), <b>ice</b> (cold, translucent and cracked), <b>lava</b> (dark rock over glowing grooves), <b>gem</b> (faceted, a deep color with flashes of the spectrum), <b>pearl</b> (soft white with a play of colors), <b>clay</b> (matte, the light wrapping round), <b>toon</b> (three tones and an ink outline), <b>glitter</b> (flecks that twinkle), <b>wireframe</b> (only the outlines and a fine mesh, on black), <b>xray</b> (the slopes bright and the flat dim, on black). The first color is the grout or the surface, the next the classes of shapes. Far away, where a tile is smaller than a pixel, every material fades into its own average color.</DocText>

## Cubes, an old illusion

<DocText>Three rhombi in a hexagon, shaded light, middle and dark, read as a cube - the tumbling-blocks quilt. In CSS the left faces are the ink and the right faces the accent; on the GPU the faces are lit as three planes, and with the isometric projection the whole floor turns with them.</DocText>
