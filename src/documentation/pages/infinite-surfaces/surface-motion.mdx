<PageHeader slug="surface-motion" title="Animated Tiles" component="surface">
  A moving pattern looks endless only when its loop has no seam. Every CSS motion of the <DocLink href="surface.html">Surface</DocLink> moves its plane by whole tiles or returns to where it started; the <DocLink href="shader.html">Shader</DocLink>'s <code>surface</code> effect moves an endless plane, so any distance works. Both stop under <code>prefers-reduced-motion</code>.
</PageHeader>

## In CSS

<DocText>23 motions. They move the plane with a transform - or its opacity, or a filter - so a running motion repaints nothing; a receding plane drifts by moving its tile, so its fade toward the horizon stays where it is. <code>--surface-duration</code> sets the loop (12s), <code>--surface-direction</code> where a drift runs (30deg, counterclockwise from the right; on a floor, a ceiling or a wall the default runs toward you).</DocText>

## On the GPU

<DocText>The surface effect moves the plane itself - 24 motions: <b>drift</b> (slides the plane in data-direction (default); on a floor, in a corridor or a tunnel the camera moves on), <b>none</b> (stands still), <b>pulse</b> (a wave of growing and shrinking shapes), <b>shimmer</b> (a highlight sweeps across), <b>ripple</b> (rings from the pointer), <b>rotate</b> (the view turns), <b>warp</b> (the plane bends like heat haze), <b>mutate</b> (the cells re-roll one by one), <b>orbit</b> (the view circles), <b>zoom</b> (zooms in and out), <b>spiral</b> (turns faster further out), <b>wave</b> (a wave runs across, lit like cloth), <b>breathe</b> (grows a little and back), <b>swirl</b> (a vortex winds and unwinds), <b>rain</b> (a head falls down each column, a fading trail behind it - with glyphs, digital rain), <b>cascade</b> (the cells rise and sink in a diagonal wave), <b>flip</b> (every cell turns over now and then), <b>spin</b> (every cell turns on its own), <b>sparkle</b> (glints flash here and there), <b>cycle</b> (the colors run through the palette), <b>glitch</b> (bands jump sideways in bursts), <b>scan</b> (a bright line runs down the picture), <b>domino</b> (the cells change color in a wave), <b>shake</b> (a short tremor now and then).</DocText>

## Structural mutation

<DocText>With <code>mutate</code> every cell keeps its own beat and re-rolls when it comes: truchet arcs turn, maze walls flip, circuit traces rewire, a dot changes its color. Because an edge of the circuit belongs to both cells it joins, a rewired trace still meets its neighbors. Slow it down with <code>data-speed</code> for a living background that never draws attention.</DocText>

```html example label="A circuit that rewires" schema="none" hint="Every cell re-rolls on its own beat."
<div class="grid gap-4" style="grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(16rem, 1fr))">
  <div
    class="shader"
    id="circuit-rewire"
    data-effect="surface"
    data-pattern="circuit"
    data-material="neon"
    data-motion="mutate"
    data-colors="#04110b #36f1a1 #f9f871"
    data-scale="12"
    data-speed="1.5"
    aria-hidden="true"
    style="--shader-aspect: 16 / 10; border-radius: var(--radius)"
  ></div>
  <div
    class="shader-controls"
    data-shader-for="circuit-rewire"
    data-controls="options params colors speed actions"
  ></div>
</div>
```
