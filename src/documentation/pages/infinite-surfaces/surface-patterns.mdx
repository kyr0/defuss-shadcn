  The flat patterns every interface reaches for, as seamless CSS tiles of the <DocLink href="surface.html">Surface</DocLink> - cheap, crisp at any size, colored by the theme. ## Never the same twice

<DocText>A tile repeats - which is right for paper and parquet, and visible in a circuit board or a maze. The surface shader computes truchet arcs, maze walls, circuit traces and contour lines per cell from a hash of the cell, so the plane never repeats. A circuit edge is on or off for both cells it joins, so traces always meet. <code>data-seed</code> draws another arrangement.</DocText>

## Every shader pattern

<DocText>The surface effect draws 26 patterns. Ten joined the first sixteen: <b>stripes</b> (bands in two colors with a groove between them), <b>chevron</b> (the stripes folded into zigzags), <b>circles</b> (rings in every cell), <b>octagons</b> (octagons with a small square between every four), <b>stars</b> (five-pointed stars, every other row moved half a cell), <b>stones</b> (rounded cobbles of a moving Voronoi net), <b>cells</b> (the raised borders of an organic net), <b>glyphs</b> (a 5 x 7 character in every cell), <b>crosses</b> (plus signs in two colors), <b>lattice</b> (a diagonal trellis with a stud at every crossing). Stones and cells are a Voronoi net of one moving point per cell, glyphs a 5 x 7 character mirrored so it reads like one - with the <code>rain</code> motion, digital rain.</DocText>
