<PageHeader slug="opening-hours" title="Opening Hours" component="opening-hours">
  Displays operating hours and date-specific exceptions - the week with today marked, whether it is open now, and the holidays. Part of the <DocLink href="website-events-promotions.html">Events & Promotions</DocLink> blocks.
</PageHeader>

<SkillPanel component="opening-hours">
  <SkillField label="Native basis">
<SkillText>A <code>&lt;section&gt;</code>: a status line, a <code>&lt;dl&gt;</code> of day / hours rows (today with <code>aria-current="date"</code>, closed days with <code>data-closed</code>) and a list of exceptions with <code>&lt;time&gt;</code>s.</SkillText>
  </SkillField>

  <SkillField label="Web Platform APIs">
<SkillRow>
    <ApiPill href="https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Element/dl" title="term / value pairs"><code>&lt;dl&gt;</code></ApiPill>

    <ApiPill href="https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Element/time" title="machine-readable dates"><code>&lt;time&gt;</code></ApiPill>
  </SkillRow>
  </SkillField>

```html example label="Default" hint="A status line (open now, and the next change), the week as a <dl> - today marked with aria-current=&quot;date&quot;, closed days muted - and the exceptions with <time>s." previewStyle="align-items:stretch;"
<section class="mk-opening-hours" aria-labelledby="oh-title">
  <h3 class="mk-opening-hours-title" id="oh-title">Opening hours</h3>
  <p class="mk-opening-hours-status">Open now <span>· until 20:00</span></p>
  <dl class="mk-opening-hours-days">
      <div><dt>Monday</dt><dd>09:00 – 18:00</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Tuesday</dt><dd>09:00 – 18:00</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Wednesday</dt><dd>09:00 – 18:00</dd></div>
      <div aria-current="date"><dt>Thursday</dt><dd>09:00 – 20:00</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Friday</dt><dd>09:00 – 18:00</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Saturday</dt><dd>10:00 – 14:00</dd></div>
      <div data-closed><dt>Sunday</dt><dd>Closed</dd></div>
    </dl>
  <ul class="mk-opening-hours-exceptions" aria-label="Exceptions">
    <li><time datetime="2026-12-24">24 Dec</time><span>09:00 – 13:00</span></li>
    <li><time datetime="2026-12-25">25–26 Dec</time><span>Closed</span></li>
    <li><time datetime="2027-01-01">1 Jan</time><span>Closed</span></li>
  </ul>
</section>
```
