<PageHeader slug="search-result" title="Search Result" component="search-result">
  Displays one matching entry with relevant context - where it lives, the title and a snippet with the matches highlighted, as text, with a thumbnail or as a product. Part of the <DocLink href="website-search-navigation.html">Search & Navigation</DocLink> blocks.
</PageHeader>

  </SkillField>
