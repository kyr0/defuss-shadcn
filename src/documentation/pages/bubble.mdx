<PageHeader slug="bubble" title="Bubble" component="bubble">
  The surface of a chat message. Bubbles size to their content, sit on the received (start) or sent (end) side, stack into groups whose joined corners tighten, grow a tail, carry overlapped emoji reactions, turn into links or buttons, fold long text behind "Show more" - and show when the other side is typing. Avatars, names and timestamps belong to the message row around it. CSS only, after shadcn/ui's Bubble.
</PageHeader>
