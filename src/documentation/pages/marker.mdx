<PageHeader slug="marker" title="Marker" component="marker">
  The quiet lines between the messages of a conversation: an inline status ("Explored 4 files"), a step in progress with a spinner, streaming "Thinking..." text, a bordered row, or a labeled separator ("Today"). An icon slot and the words - muted, so they never compete with the <DocLink href="bubble.html">bubbles</DocLink>. CSS only, after shadcn/ui's Marker.
</PageHeader>

<SkillPanel component="marker">
  <SkillField label="Native basis">
<SkillText>A <code>&lt;div class="marker"&gt;</code> - or an <code>&lt;a&gt;</code> / <code>&lt;button&gt;</code> when the row is an action - with a decorative <code>.marker-icon</code> and the <code>.marker-content</code> words; <code>role="status"</code> for progress.</SkillText>
  </SkillField>

  <SkillField label="Web Platform APIs">
<SkillRow>
    <ApiPill href="https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Accessibility/ARIA/Roles/status_role" title="announced progress"><code>role="status"</code></ApiPill>
    <ApiPill href="https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/background-clip" title="the streaming shimmer"><code>background-clip: text</code></ApiPill>
    <ApiPill href="https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/::before" title="the separator rules"><code>::before</code> / <code>::after</code></ApiPill>
    <ApiPill href="https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/@media/prefers-reduced-motion" title="no shimmer sweep"><code>prefers-reduced-motion</code></ApiPill>
  </SkillRow>
  </SkillField>

  <SkillField label="Classes">
<SkillRow>
    <code>.marker</code>
    <code>.marker-icon</code>
    <code>.marker-content</code>
  </SkillRow>
  </SkillField>

  <SkillField label="Data attributes">
<SkillText><code>data-variant</code> (<code>border</code>, <code>separator</code>), <code>data-orientation="vertical"</code>, <code>data-tone</code> (<code>success</code>, <code>warning</code>, <code>info</code>, <code>destructive</code>, <code>primary</code>), <code>data-size</code> (<code>sm</code>, <code>lg</code>); on the content: <code>data-shimmer</code>.</SkillText>
  </SkillField>
</SkillPanel>
