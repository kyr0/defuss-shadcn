    <h2 id="rs-abstract">Abstract</h2>
    <p>Redeschrift 1.0 transcribes German speech on an ordinary processor, offline and private. It is an ONNX export of a German fine-tune (primeline/parakeet-primeline) of NVIDIA's Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3, compressed into three shipping variants. On a German evaluation corpus of 9,799 utterances the word error rate is 3.27 % for the smallest variant (672 MiB) and 3.12 % for the largest, against 3.02 % for the uncompressed reference - one error in roughly thirty spoken words.</p>

    <h2 id="rs-s1"><span class="paper-num">1</span>Variants</h2>

  <section class="paper-section" aria-labelledby="rs-s2">
    <h2 id="rs-s2"><span class="paper-num">2</span>Gates</h2>
    <p>Three gates measure a variant: a smoke test of 7 hand-picked recordings (89 words), a stratified set of 980 utterances and the full German corpus of 9,799. A gate fails when the variant's word error rate exceeds the reference's by more than 0.25 points. M passes the 980 set and the full corpus. S passes the full corpus by three thousandths of a point - labelled borderline - and missed the 980 set against its wider baseline, a trade made for the smaller download. L matches the reference on the 980 set; the full corpus was not run for it. Of the alternatives, a 4-bit block quantization failed both corpora, per-channel uint8 changed nothing measurable, and dynamic uint8 - the widest miss - serves only as the app's fallback until the 4-bit runtime is validated.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="paper-section" aria-labelledby="rs-s3">
    <h2 id="rs-s3"><span class="paper-num">3</span>Use</h2>
    <p>All variants run in sherpa-onnx with ONNX Runtime on a standard CPU - no graphics card, no network. Besides German, the model transcribes 24 more European languages, among them English, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish and Russian; speech that switches into English mid-sentence is supported. The model is released under CC BY 4.0; dialect support is planned for version 2.</p>
  </section>
