<PageHeader slug="sort-control" title="Sort Control" component="sort-control">
  Changes the ordering of a collection - a labelled select, a segmented choice, or a button that opens a menu of options. Part of the <DocLink href="website-search-navigation.html">Search & Navigation</DocLink> blocks.
</PageHeader>

<SkillPanel component="sort-control">
  <SkillField label="Native basis">
<SkillText>A labelled <code>&lt;select name="sort"&gt;</code>; or a radio group styled as segments; or a button with <code>popovertarget</code> opening a <code>popover</code> of radios (the menu stays a form control).</SkillText>
  </SkillField>

  <SkillField label="Built from">
<SkillText><DocLink href="select.html">Select</DocLink>, <DocLink href="button.html">Button</DocLink>.</SkillText>
  </SkillField>

<SkillRow>

```html example label="Default" hint="A visible &quot;Sort by&quot; label and a native Select." previewStyle="align-items:stretch;"
<div class="mk-sort-control"><label for="so-1">Sort by</label><select class="select" id="so-1" name="sort"><option>Most relevant</option><option>Newest</option><option>Price: low to high</option><option>Price: high to low</option></select></div>
```

```html example label="Segmented" hint="data-variant='segmented': a radio group styled as segments." previewStyle="align-items:stretch;"
<fieldset class="mk-sort-control" data-variant="segmented"><legend>Sort by</legend><label><input type="radio" name="sort2" value="popular" checked>Popular</label><label><input type="radio" name="sort2" value="newest">Newest</label><label><input type="radio" name="sort2" value="price">Price</label></fieldset>
```

```html example label="Menu" hint="data-variant='menu': a button (popovertarget) opens a popover of radios anchored to it - Escape or a click outside closes it; the choice is a real form value." previewStyle="align-items:stretch;"
<div class="mk-sort-control" data-variant="menu">
  <button class="btn" data-variant="outline" data-size="sm" type="button" popovertarget="so-menu" style="anchor-name:--so-menu"><i data-lucide="arrow-up-down"></i> Newest</button>
  <div class="mk-sort-control-menu" id="so-menu" popover style="position-anchor:--so-menu" role="radiogroup" aria-label="Sort by">
    <label><input type="radio" name="sort3" value="0">Most relevant</label>
    <label><input type="radio" name="sort3" value="1" checked>Newest</label>
    <label><input type="radio" name="sort3" value="2">Price: low to high</label>
    <label><input type="radio" name="sort3" value="3">Price: high to low</label>
  </div>
</div>
```
