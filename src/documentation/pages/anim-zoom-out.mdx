Zoom Out is the shrink-in entrance of the motion vocabulary (`zoom-out` in `motion.css`): the element starts larger than its final size and settles at scale 1. Imperatively it ships as the paired `zoomIn` / `zoomOut` channels - playing `zoomIn` with `scale: 1.1` starts the element at 110% and lands at 100%, while `zoomOut` with the same scale grows the element toward 110% as it fades away. The pair is not directional: it scales around an `origin` instead of traveling from an edge. Everything below is driven by `df$.anim.zoomIn.*()` / `df$.anim.zoomOut.*()` on the shared animation engine.

The scroller above is a local container; pass `{ target: 'viewport' }` instead to bind progress to the page's own scroll position - the parallax use for presentations and long-form pages.

## Configuration

The zoom pair accepts these options on `play(el, opts)`:

- `origin` - the `transform-origin` the scale grows or shrinks around (default `'50% 50%'`).
- `scale` - the scale factor at the far end of the animation (default `0.8`; `1.1` here gives the shrink-in entrance for `zoomIn` and the grow-away exit for `zoomOut`).
- `duration` - total duration in ms (default `1500`).
- `easing` - any CSS easing (default `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
