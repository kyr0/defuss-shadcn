<PageHeader slug="fab" title="FAB" component="fab">
  A floating action button - the one main action of a screen, always in reach - and its speed dial: a few related actions that fan out from it. The dial is a native <code>popover</code>, so opening, Escape, clicking outside and <code>aria-expanded</code> are the browser's; CSS anchor positioning stacks the actions at the trigger. No JavaScript.
</PageHeader>
