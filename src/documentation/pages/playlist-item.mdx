<PageHeader slug="playlist-item" title="Playlist Item" component="playlist-item">
  Represents one selectable track or episode within a playlist - number, cover, title, artist and length; the selected one shows "now playing" bars. Part of the <DocLink href="website-media.html">Media</DocLink> blocks.
</PageHeader>
