<PageHeader slug="navbar" title="Navbar" component="navbar">
  The top bar of an app or a site: a <code>&lt;nav&gt;</code> with start, center and end regions for the brand, the primary links and the actions - buttons, search, an account dropdown, a cart indicator. The responsive menu is <strong>one</strong> native popover: inline on wide screens, opened by a toggle on narrow ones. No JavaScript. The header of this site is the same pattern.
</PageHeader>
