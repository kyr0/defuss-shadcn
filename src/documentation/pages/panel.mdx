<PageHeader slug="panel" title="Panel" component="panel">
  A title bar inside a <DocLink href="card.html">Card</DocLink> - an optional icon, the title and small tools on the right. Minimize and maximize are <DocLink href="swap.html">Swaps</DocLink>: a checkbox each, the faces swap without script. Minimized, the card goes and only the title bar stays. Put panels into a <DocLink href="border-layout.html">Border Layout</DocLink> and they take their region with them, the way the ExtJS 4 border layout did: north and south shrink to the title bar, west and east turn it into a vertical tab with the icon, title and restore tool still showing. Maximized, a panel fills its layout (or the viewport); Escape brings it back.
</PageHeader>

<StatesSection>
  <p class="text-sm text-muted-foreground m-0 mb-3">Named states via the shared State API, driven per panel through the bound <code>api</code>:</p>
  <ul class="text-sm text-muted-foreground m-0 mb-3 ps-5">
    <li><code>default</code> - title bar and body at the authored size</li>
    <li><code>minimized</code> - the title bar only; in a border layout region the region folds with it (a vertical tab in west / east)</li>
    <li><code>maximized</code> - fills its border layout, <code>[data-panel-host]</code> or the viewport</li>
    <li><code>closed</code> - gone (<code>hidden</code>); in a border layout its region goes with it - a <code>.panel-close</code> tool closes, a <code>[data-panel-open]</code> button opens</li>
  </ul>
  <p class="text-sm text-muted-foreground">The first example carries <code>data-state-demo</code>; <code>bun run screenshots</code> drives it via <code>el.api.setState(name)</code> and captures <code>screenshots/\{mode\}/panel-\{state\}.png</code>.</p>
