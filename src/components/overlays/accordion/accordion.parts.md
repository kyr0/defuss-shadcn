```html example part label="Accordion (lg)"
<div class="accordion" data-size="lg" data-marker="arrow" style="width: 100%">
  <details class="accordion-item" open>
    <summary class="accordion-trigger"><span>Is it accessible?</span></summary>
    <div class="accordion-content"
      ><p>Yes - native details/summary: keyboard and screen readers work out of the box.</p></div
    >
  </details>
  <details class="accordion-item">
    <summary class="accordion-trigger"><span>Is it styled?</span></summary>
    <div class="accordion-content"><p>It follows your theme tokens - every variant below is one attribute.</p></div>
  </details>
</div>
```
