Of the Art of Printing

Printing is the art of multiplying writing by movable type: the compositor sets each letter, justifies each line and locks the forme before a single sheet is pulled. Ligatures such as fi, fl and ct, and old-style figures like 1468, belong to the craft.

Each following paragraph runs on without a gap; only its first line steps in by one em, the classic indent of a book page, so the column reads as one even grey.

A justified column hyphenates long words like typographically, interdisciplinary and internationalization, and its last line stays flush to the start.

A column of twenty rem is narrower than two columns of eighteen, so the block sets a single column - the responsive case, with no media query.
