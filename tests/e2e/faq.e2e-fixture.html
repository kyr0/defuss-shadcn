FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know about plans, data and getting started.

What is Acme?

A calm workspace where your team plans, writes and ships - roadmaps, docs and tasks on one surface.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Starter plan is free for up to three people, forever. Upgrade when your team grows.

Can I import from other tools?

One-click importers move your projects from Notion, Jira, Linear and Asana - comments and attachments included.

How does Acme handle my data?

Everything is encrypted in transit and at rest, hosted in the EU, and never used to train models.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?

Nonprofits and schools get 50% off any paid plan - write to us from your organization's address.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes - plans are monthly or yearly, and you can export everything before you leave.
