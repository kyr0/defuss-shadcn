Hair Salon

A one-page site for a hair studio - prices that change with the length of your hair and the stylist's level, colour with its processing times, four stylists, a booking flow with the free slots of each day, a before and after of a balayage, a look book, products to put aside, gift cards and the opening hours. Three looks: Salon, Neon and Botanical.

Open full screen

Juniper & Shear, hair studio

The Look select switches palette, typeface and how the pinned note, the circled word and the stamp are drawn: Salon (ivory and terracotta, Bodoni), Neon (night violet and hot pink, Syne) and Botanical (sage paper, Fraunces). Length and level radios reprice the whole menu; the booking form lists the free slots per day and stylist and ends in a confirmation dialog. The before and after is the Diff component, the look book opens the Media Gallery's viewer.

×%

Built from Site Header (its menu a Sheet on narrow screens), Radio cards, Badge, Team Member, Booking Form with Select, Input and Textarea, a Diff for the before and after, Gallery Items opening the Media Gallery's viewer, Cards, Opening Hours and Dialogs for the confirmations, the imprint and the privacy notes. The prices, the free slots and the next free appointment come from a few lines of page script; the palettes are tokens on the site's root, so every component inside follows the look.
