Spa

A one-page site for a day spa in an old bathhouse - a pool window of moving light, ten treatments with their minutes and prices behind a filter, day packages, a booking that names the therapist and the slot, a gift voucher that writes itself as you type, every room on one thermometer from 12 to 90 °C and the quiet hours, live. Three looks: Stone, Eucalyptus and Night bath. One of the Showcase sites.

Open full screen

Salt & Cedar, a day spa

The Look select switches the palette, the water in the pool window and the seal on the voucher: Stone (limestone and basalt), Eucalyptus (sage and deep green) and Night bath (ink blue and candle light). The pool window is a Shader (caustics), the sign with today's temperatures hangs from a string, the filter is radio chips read by :has(), the booking and the voucher send to a thank-you dialog that resets the form when it closes.

×%

Built from Site Header (its Sheet on a phone), Hero (split), Section Header, Filter Bar chips, Pricing, Booking Form, Testimonials, Opening Hours, Site Footer, Avatar, Select, Input, Textarea, Radio, Swap and four Dialogs. The pool window is a Shader (caustics) in a corner-arch, the voucher a ticket (Shapes); the thermometer, the duration rings and the hanging sign are the page's own CSS. The palettes are tokens on the site's root, so every component inside follows the look.
