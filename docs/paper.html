§Verified Agentic Engineering in Practice - a technical report

The whole page is one .paper article: the title block with a light/dark Swap, a news line, the teaser (an Illustrative Diagram that plays itself), the abstract, a table of contents, twelve numbered sections with findings, epistemic tags (VAE-DIALECT), key numbers (Statistic), six charts (Chart: growth, activity, lines of code, size distribution, the four changes, cache use), tables (Table), a quotation, two BibTeX blocks and the footer. Current figures are data-stat elements - written from dist/stats.json and the recorded runs, checked by verify; historical figures come from git and the issue tracker, dated. Every numbered section is set as Typography's Book page without an initial: Blocksatz, Einzug, classic ligatures (.paper-section.typeset).

×%

§Variants

data-variant on the .paper sets a house style over the same parts: classic is a printed journal (serif throughout, a narrower justified column, small-caps section heads, a plain abstract), minimal a lab note (flush start, a sans title, no tint, no rules). The Paper Gallery shows each in a whole short paper.

§classic and minimal

The same title, abstract and section in the two other house styles - classic on the left, minimal on the right.

×%

§Two columns (composition)

A classic two-column body is Typography's .typeset in the paper's wide track - data-columns='2', Blocksatz, Einzug. Switch the preview to Phone: below two columns of 18rem it sets one.
